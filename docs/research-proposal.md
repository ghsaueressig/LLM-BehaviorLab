# Preliminary research proposal

## Working title

Analysis of consistency and instruction influence in Large Language Model responses to personality assessment instruments.

## Research question

To what extent do different Large Language Models provide consistent responses to personality questionnaires, and how do changes in the instructions provided to these models influence the results?

## General objective

Develop a computational tool to automate experiments with LLMs, enabling the analysis of response consistency and the influence of different instructions on generated responses.

## Scope note

The research examines observable patterns in model outputs. It does not claim that LLMs possess human personality or that questionnaire scores constitute psychological diagnoses.
